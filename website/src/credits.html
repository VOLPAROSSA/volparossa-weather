Sources and credits

Forecast products

The Berlin plate adapts ECMWF Open Data under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The decoder selected the nearest grid point and converted air temperature from kelvin to Celsius. Inspect the archived model record.

Fine-particle forecasts come from NOAA GEFS-Aerosols. The model supplies PM2.5 and PM10 concentrations on its own timeline. NOAA terms of use.

Atlas plates

Coastlines use Natural Earth public-domain geography, 1:50m land, v5.1.2, clipped and projected for this atlas. The fronts, isobars, devices and delivery paths are illustrations. The engraved storm, fox and cloud studies were generated for this page from the supplied visual direction.
