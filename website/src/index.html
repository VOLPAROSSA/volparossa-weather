One forecast, several readers.

A publisher can share a forecast for a chosen public area. Peers can deliver the same product to other readers. The model run, units and valid times stay with the data.

How readers check a forecast

The reader checks the trusted publisher, file integrity and expiry. Reading a cached copy keeps the original forecast age. Point selection uses model fields locally. The owner explicitly chooses a public area to publish.

What do the numbers mean?

Temperature{{thermometer}} °C
Air temperature at 2 m, for the model’s stated valid time.
Wind{{wind}} m/s
Wind speed at 10 m. Direction is the bearing the wind comes from. At zero speed, direction is undefined.
Precipitation{{rain}} mm
Total over the model’s stated accumulation window. Read both times with the amount. Windows can overlap.
Particles{{particles}} µg/m³
NOAA GEFS-Aerosols supplies forecast PM2.5 and PM10 concentrations at its own valid times.

Research direction

How could forecasts improve?

A forecast can be compared with a later observation for the same place, time and variable. The project aims to test whether a correction improves on the original model in other places and periods.
